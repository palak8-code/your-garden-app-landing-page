Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Your Garden App is designed to help you organise and care for your plants. We aim to handle your information thoughtfully and transparently.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, information may include your name and email address for account authentication, as well as plant information and care activities you choose to record.

How information is used

Information is used to provide app features such as account access, plant organisation, care schedules, reminders, and related plant-care guidance.

Storage and security

App information may be stored using cloud services with security measures intended to protect it, including access controls and encryption where supported by the underlying service.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is not shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your choices and account deletion

You can manage information you enter in the app. If account deletion is available in your app settings, use that option to request deletion. You may also contact the app publisher through the Google Play listing for support.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Please review this page periodically for the latest version.
